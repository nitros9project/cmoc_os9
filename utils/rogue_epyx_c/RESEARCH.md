## Historical Background

### Glenn Wichman's Rogue History

- Link:
  https://web.archive.org/web/20150217024917/http://www.wichman.org/roguehistory.html
- Title: "A Brief History of Rogue"
- Author: Glenn R. Wichman
- Original copyright date shown on page: 1997
- Archived capture used: February 17, 2015

Notes:

- First-person account by one of Rogue's original creators.
- Covers the curses-based origin of Rogue, Michael Toy and Glenn Wichman's
  early work, the later Berkeley/BSD connection with Ken Arnold, and Rogue's
  spread through university UNIX systems.
- Mentions A.I.Design and Epyx taking over marketing and packaging of the
  commercial game after the IBM PC and Macintosh versions.
- Useful as provenance/context for the CoCo 3 Epyx port, but not an
  implementation reference for CoCo 3 behavior.
